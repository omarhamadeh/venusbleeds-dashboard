>
> I grew up watching women in my family navigate choices that were never simple. and none of them needed a European framework to tell them what liberation meant.
>
> that's what the six seconds were about.
>
> *(pause.)*

**Caption:** `said it in 6 seconds. took me a week to say the rest.`

### Voice confirmed for P2 this session
The first two script drafts were rejected for sounding too structured, too lecture-y, too AI. The confirmed voice is:
- Opens by grounding in something real that happened: "so I posted a video last week..."
- Thinking out loud, not delivering a thesis
- Acknowledges the complication before making the point: "and I want to be careful here. because yes — [the thing]. but..."
- Sharpest line lands mid-script, not at the end — it's a beat inside conversation
- Ends by trailing off, not landing: "anyway. that's what it was actually about."
- Never performs anger. Witnessing, not accusing.
