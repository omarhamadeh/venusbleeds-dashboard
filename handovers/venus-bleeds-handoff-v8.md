## HOOK MECHANICS — THE FOUR

**1. INCOMPLETE TRUTH**
Sentence that cannot resolve without the next. Short, clean, max two clauses.
✅ "I used to think the loneliness was mine. then I found out it had a name — and the name is going to make you angry."
❌ Over-worded, self-resolving sentences

**2. NAMED STAKE**
Announce something specific is about to be said that most people won't say. Never "most people get X wrong" — preachy.
✅ "I have one true thing to say about the Lebanese diaspora in Paris that nobody in that community wants to hear."

**3. DIRECT ADDRESS**
Name a SPECIFIC experience, not a general emotion. Must be something your audience has lived but never heard named.
✅ "if you've spent your whole life shapeshifting to survive every room you walk into — and you're starting to forget which shape is actually yours — stay."
❌ "if you've ever forgotten what okay feels like" — too soft, too vague

**4. INTERRUPTED DELIVERY (P2 only)**
Sentence cuts before the answer. The pause IS the hook. Subject must be charged.
✅ "white gay men do this specific thing when they want you and—"
✅ "the Lebanese diaspora in Paris has a dirty secret that nobody wants to say and—"

**7–9 SECOND RULE**
Urgency earned in first 2 seconds must be re-earned at second 8. Build a second beat — pivot, new detail, register shift.
